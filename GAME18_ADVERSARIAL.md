## P10-D Resolve budgets

STRUCTURAL prevents spam and normally leaves >=2 beats slack. BEAT_PUZZLE explicitly makes beat economy the proof shape and requires solver witnesses plus an understandable near-miss. Cases 17 and 35 are explicit beat-economy anchors. No-change Resolve consumes one beat and never accumulates duration.
