# 1. Commercial baseline

**Premium one-time purchase.**

No:
- ads;
- gacha;
- energy systems;
- battle pass;
- live-service progression;
- paid puzzle hints;
- progression currency;
- consumable retries;
- move-limit monetization;
- mandatory account/login for single-player use.

The shipped campaign is complete offline-capable single-player content.

# 3. Price strategy

## Working launch target
**$19.99 USD base price** is the current design assumption.

## Acceptable pre-release decision band
**$17.99–$21.99** if production polish, playtime evidence, regional-price strategy, wishlist conversion or publisher/store context justify adjustment.

A move to **$24.99** should require materially stronger evidence of presentation value, polish and perceived breadth rather than simply adding filler encounters.

Final commercial price remains a release-stage empirical/business decision, not gameplay canon.

### Price/value promise
At $19.99 working target, the product must offer:
- a distinctive mechanic visible within seconds;
- 24–28 carefully authored main encounters rather than repeated filler;
- roughly 4–6 hours first completion for typical puzzle pace;
- controller + keyboard/mouse parity;
- polished deterministic restart/checkpoint flow;
- strong accessibility baseline;
- a representative free demo.

---

# 4. Demo funnel

The free Steam demo is a central discovery tool, not a detached tutorial.

## Demo content
Use the Phase-5 four-encounter compressed package:
1. two-load give/take;
2. TAUT middle compromise;
3. traversal-separated reconfiguration;
4. load mutation + return inversion.

Target first-time length: **15–25 minutes**, preferably ~18–24.

## Demo ending
End immediately after the player experiences the true mature hook: the rig mutates, the old configuration changes meaning, and extraction requires a new compromise.

Then show:
- full-game promise in one concise panel/trailer beat;
- wishlist / store navigation CTA where platform policy allows;
- no artificial cliffhanger that misrepresents the puzzle campaign.

## Demo parity
Demo uses final-intent controls, camera, visual state grammar and accessibility model. It must not be a simplified mechanic variant.

---

# 5. Steam festival / release positioning

Use Steam demo events / Next Fest only when the vertical slice already communicates the full differentiator. Prior discovery research established that festival visibility is crowded and should not be treated as a rescue mechanism.

Pre-festival gate:
- mute 8–10 second clip communicates one pull changing several loads;
- unfamiliar testers understand give/take without numbers;
- demo reaches mutation/return inversion in target duration;
- controller experience is credible;
- store capsule/title/art direction can communicate a physical rig rather than generic engineering.

Do not burn the strongest demo event on an early tutorial-only build.

---

# 6. Progression model

There is **no player power progression**.

Campaign progression is knowledge/mastery:
- encounters unlock in a mostly guided sequence;
- completed encounters remain replayable;
- later bands introduce new combinations of frozen rules, not upgrades;
- no currency gates the next puzzle.

Recommended structure:
- Band A mostly linear onboarding;
- after core literacy, small 2–3 encounter branches may allow pacing choice;
- synthesis encounters require enough preceding completions to preserve teaching order;
- player never grinds solved content to unlock mechanics.

Exact level-map presentation belongs to later whole-game/technical work but must preserve simple resume/replay behavior.

---

# 7. Retention philosophy

The product is not designed for daily retention metrics. Desired retention is:
- “one more puzzle” session momentum;
- curiosity about how the same three load families recombine;
- satisfaction from solving without dexterity tax;
- replay of favorite encounters / optional mastery goals;
- sharing short causal clips.

Session target remains roughly 20–60 minutes depending on player puzzle pace, with no daily login reward.

---

# 8. Replay incentives

Baseline replay value may come from:
1. replaying completed encounters freely;
2. optional discovery of alternate valid solution sequences where authored/validated;
3. achievements tied to understanding/mastery rather than grind;
4. optional 4–6 mastery remixes only if repetition testing supports them.

Avoid move-count stars as a core progression system. If a later optional challenge records personal best commit count, it must not imply that exploratory play was “wrong” or punish accessibility use.

---

# 9. Achievement architecture

Target roughly **18–28 achievements**, with final count decided after content lock.

Families:
- campaign milestones;
- first successful TAUT middle-state solution;
- first mutation/return inversion completion;
- solve selected mature encounter without unnecessary restart, only if this remains accessibility-neutral;
- discover validated alternate solution in selected encounter;
- complete optional mastery content if retained;
- accessibility/settings use must never disable achievements.

No grind achievements requiring hundreds of carriage moves or repeated completions.

---

# 10. Optional mastery content boundary

Phase 5 permits at most **4–6 optional remixes** after the 26 main encounters if empirical repetition evidence remains good.

Commercially:
- these are included in the base game;
- they are not DLC bait;
- they are first cut if they dilute overall quality;
- store-hour claims must not depend on them.

Future paid expansion is not planned in the baseline. If the game succeeds, an expansion would require genuinely new coherent design work rather than withheld 1.0 puzzles.

---

# 11. Soundtrack / extras

Optional soundtrack sale may be considered if original music production justifies it; it is commercially separate and mechanically irrelevant.

No gameplay-affecting deluxe edition.

Cosmetic extras are not required because the player/avatar is not the retention pillar.

---

# 12. Regional pricing / discount philosophy

Final regional pricing follows current Steam/publisher guidance at release time rather than a frozen 2026 table.

Discount philosophy:
- no need for deep launch discount to compensate for weak value proposition;
- modest launch discount may be tested commercially;
- later seasonal discounts are normal premium lifecycle behavior;
- pricing decisions must not trigger gameplay/content inflation.

---

# 13. Store positioning

Primary store sentence should emphasize physical simultaneous consequence, not abstract “tension management.”

Design-authority direction:
**Move one tension carriage and the whole rig answers: lifts rise, gates give way and spans sag as you build a temporary route through a suspended mechanical complex.**

Store tags likely center around:
- Puzzle;
- Puzzle Adventure / Logic where accurate;
- Singleplayer;
- Indie;
- Atmospheric/Stylized if final art supports them.

Do not lead with `Simulation`, `Automation`, `Physics Sandbox`, `Factory`, or `Engineering` if they misrepresent the authored deterministic product.

---

# 14. Commercial kill signals

Before committing to release-scale content/polish, reconsider commercial positioning if:
- demo players understand the rule but do not find the carriage interaction satisfying;
- store clip reads as generic “move platform” puzzle rather than simultaneous redistribution;
- 15–25 minute demo cannot reach mature differentiation without rushing onboarding;
- playtime can only reach target by retaining validator-flagged repetitive encounters;
- market collision emerges with a direct near-identical product before launch.

The response is re-evaluation, not adding currencies, enemies or gadgets.

---

# 15. Phase 7 frozen decisions

- premium buy-once baseline;
- working launch target **$19.99 USD**;
- pre-release decision band **$17.99–$21.99** without reopening gameplay design;
- $24.99 requires stronger polish/value evidence, not padding;
- free representative Steam demo, 15–25 min;
- no power economy/currency/grind/live service;
- knowledge/mastery progression only;
- completed encounters replayable;
- roughly 18–28 non-grindy achievements target;
- optional 4–6 remixes included in base product if retained;
- no gameplay-affecting DLC/deluxe model;
- commercial decisions may not violate the frozen scope ceiling.
