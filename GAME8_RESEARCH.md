# 1. Fresh market read — August 2026

The current Steam/indie environment rewards a legible hook but is exceptionally noisy. Recent public reporting places 2025 Steam releases above 20k titles and only a small fraction reaching meaningful review counts. This makes generic genre competence insufficient; the project needs a GIF-readable action and a product identity that can be explained without a paragraph.

Current signals reviewed:
- 2026 continues to show strong attention for cheap social/co-op titles; the recent breakout of *How to Fish* is another reminder that accessible multiplayer concepts can explode, but chasing that wave would force networking/content/community costs that the factory should not accept without a uniquely multiplayer mechanic.
- deckbuilder/roguelite hybrids remain extremely crowded. 2026 releases such as *Cascadou* and the Next Fest visibility of coin/deck/roguelite hybrids show that combining a familiar object with Balatro-like progression is now a default indie pitch, not differentiation.
- inventory-management roguelites remain active (*Inventorix* is a recent example), so "pack items + synergies + runs" is treated as saturated for this cycle.
- survival/crafting, dark-fantasy co-op and Souls-like presentation continue to occupy large portions of festival attention. These can have strong ceilings but carry asset, networking and production burdens disproportionate to this factory's desired risk profile.
- puzzle remains commercially narrower than the largest Steam genres, so a pure abstract puzzle must compensate with exceptional visual legibility, demo strength and causal depth. Game #008 is therefore allowed to move toward compact systemic simulation, occupational fantasy or management if that produces a stronger market surface.
- nostalgia-rich occupational sims can still break out when the job fantasy is instantly recognizable; *Retro Rewind* is a useful current signal. The lesson is not to clone shopkeeping, but to value concrete verbs, readable tools and stories generated by systems.

Primary opportunity thesis for this cycle:
> Seek a compact premium single-player game whose main verb looks satisfying before the rules are understood, whose causal system can generate escalating decisions without hundreds of bespoke mechanics, and whose identity is neither a current roguelite/deckbuilder reskin nor another factory portfolio variant.

Research references consulted this pass include Steam pages/current reporting for Cascadou (2026), Inventorix (2025), Steam Next Fest 2026 coverage, current 2026 indie-market reporting, and recent breakout/co-op coverage. Market claims are directional inputs, not design authority.

# 2. Hard portfolio exclusion field

Reject or heavily penalize any candidate whose central identity collapses into:
- transporting cargo/organisms as the main fantasy (#001);
- bureaucracy where representation/map/document becomes reality (#002);
- carrying/transferring a collision, consequence or physical property between targets (#003);
- audio-first inference through hidden geometry (#004);
- a conserved tension/load budget distributed through a physical network (#005);
- rewiring scarce room adjacency/topology (#006);
- authored observation committing a remembered form that later becomes physical (#007).

Also penalize this cycle:
- generic deckbuilder/roguelite progression;
- inventory-grid synergies as the main loop;
- survivor-like combat;
- extraction loops;
- generic cozy farming;
- generic shopkeeping;
- networked co-op without a mechanic that fundamentally requires another human;
- procedural content used to hide a shallow rule set;
- expensive narrative/voice acting as the primary value source.

# 3. Candidate field — 36 distinct concepts

Scores use 1–5 for: Hook (H), systemic depth (D), scope fitness (S), visual readability (V), market differentiation (M), implementation safety (I), content efficiency (C), demo strength (Demo). Maximum 40. Scores are Phase-1 screening estimates, not commitments.

## G8C01 — Maintenance Window
**Fantasy:** keep a compact automated facility alive while every machine occasionally needs to be stopped, opened and serviced.
**Core verb:** schedule and physically perform maintenance during overlapping safe windows; stopping one machine changes flow/heat/pressure elsewhere.
**Second-order depth:** preventive vs emergency repair, dependency order, temporary bypasses, accumulated wear, and deliberately creating a short outage now to prevent a cascading outage later.
**Risk:** could become spreadsheety or resemble generic factory automation if the hands-on service verb is weak.
**Score:** H5 D5 S4 V4 M4 I4 C5 Demo5 = **36/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C02 — Locksmith's Margin
**Fantasy:** hand-cut a small set of key blanks in a night locksmith workshop to satisfy a sequence of real mechanical locks.
**Core verb:** make irreversible cuts/filings, test physically, and decide when one partially compatible key should be preserved for a later lock.
**Depth:** tolerance, master-pin families, destructive correction, impression marks, reusing a key as a diagnostic object rather than an inventory build.
**Risk:** finite blank material can drift into a generic resource-budget puzzle; must remain tactile and lock-specific.
**Score:** H5 D4 S5 V5 M5 I4 C4 Demo5 = **37/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C03 — In Case of Rain
**Fantasy:** run a tiny city umbrella/rain-service booth while short local weather fronts visibly move across the street grid.
**Core verb:** position limited services before weather arrives, then decide whether to chase demand or protect stranded customers/items.
**Depth:** imperfect-but-visible forecasts, neighborhood behavior, drying/reuse lag, changing foot traffic.
**Risk:** could devolve into generic shopkeeping/resource allocation.
**Score:** H4 D4 S5 V5 M4 I4 C4 Demo4 = **34/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C04 — Window Garden
**Fantasy:** cultivate architectural plants by controlling shutters, trellises and pruning while the sun sweeps predictably through a miniature building facade.
**Core verb:** redirect light and prune growth so tomorrow's plant geometry creates useful shade/support/access.
**Depth:** growth is delayed consequence; one plant can shade another, climb authored supports, block a vent, or create a bridge for pollinators.
**Risk:** growth simulation/readability could become fuzzy; must be deterministic and visually forecastable.
**Score:** H5 D5 S4 V5 M5 I3 C5 Demo5 = **37/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C05 — Last Train Dispatcher
**Fantasy:** operate one dense rail junction at the end of service when delays, maintenance and passenger connections collide.
**Core verb:** hold, platform, short-turn or release trains at discrete control points.
**Depth:** passenger transfers, crew/platform occupation, late-train knock-on effects, optional service sacrifice to recover the network.
**Risk:** railway dispatch is an established niche; must avoid becoming a timetable spreadsheet.
**Score:** H4 D5 S4 V4 M3 I4 C5 Demo4 = **33/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C06 — Elevator Etiquette
**Fantasy:** manually run an old elevator in a strange but grounded apartment building where passengers have incompatible needs and social constraints.
**Core verb:** choose boarding/order/stops while cabin position, load, priority and passenger interactions alter future requests.
**Depth:** not a dialogue game; the puzzle comes from deterministic passenger rules, destination coupling and who is willing/able to share a ride.
**Risk:** can read as mobile optimization unless presentation and emergent stories are excellent.
**Score:** H5 D4 S5 V5 M5 I5 C4 Demo5 = **38/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C07 — Firebreak Foreman
**Fantasy:** protect a compact industrial/forest edge site as a visible fire advances through materials and wind lanes.
**Core verb:** pre-emptively clear, soak, open or sacrifice specific structures to shape fire spread.
**Depth:** smoke access, wind shifts declared ahead, material ignition order, intentional sacrificial burns, worker access.
**Risk:** visual fire simulation can imply continuous physics the game cannot faithfully promise.
**Score:** H5 D5 S4 V5 M4 I3 C5 Demo5 = **36/40 — SURVIVE**.

## G8C08 — Pawnshop Stories
**Fantasy:** appraise odd second-hand objects under time pressure without omniscient price tags.

**Depth:** provenance patterns, counterfeits, repairability, paired objects, seller constraints, later resale feedback.
**Risk:** content-writing burden and similarity to appraisal/shop sims; systemic evidence generator must carry most of the load.

# 5. Cross-candidate destructive questions for Phase 2

Every survivor must answer the same questions with concrete gameplay, not pitch language:

1. **15-second trailer proof:** what exact before/action/after sequence can a viewer understand without narration?
2. **Minute 5:** what decision is the player making after basic controls are known?
3. **Hour 3:** what new reasoning exists that is not simply more entities/more meters/faster timing?
4. **Mature blueprint:** provide at least three specific difficult scenarios using the same primitive vocabulary.
5. **Dominant strategy attack:** identify the cheapest policy and author a case where it fails for causal—not arbitrary—reasons.
6. **State readability:** can the player explain why the system changed without reading logs or hidden numeric tables?
7. **No-spreadsheet gate:** if an optimal player is likely to externalize the game into a table, what physical/spatial state makes that unnecessary or undesirable?
8. **Content cost:** can 25–35 strong situations be produced mostly from recombination/data rather than bespoke assets/scripts?
9. **Demo arc:** can a 15–30 minute demo show the hook, one reversal, and one surprising second-order use?
10. **Portfolio contamination:** does the candidate secretly reduce to cargo routing, representation authority, transferable properties, audio inference, conserved network budget, adjacency rewiring or observation memory?
11. **Market adjacency:** name the closest current products and state the exact mechanical boundary, not just a theme difference.
12. **Implementation falsifier:** what prototype result would kill the concept before bulk content production?

# 6. Candidate-specific Phase-2 attack priorities

### G8C06 Elevator Etiquette
Prove it is not a mobile floor-ordering puzzle. Need deterministic passenger interaction rules that create state-dependent future requests and emergent stories without dialogue trees. Test whether one greedy policy (nearest stop / highest priority first) dominates.

### G8C02 Locksmith's Margin
Prove key cutting is strategically legible rather than trial-and-error. Need a bounded lock model, visible evidence, and mature cases where preserving an imperfect key is rational. Kill if optimal play becomes memorizing pin patterns or conserving blank material generically.

### G8C04 Window Garden
Prove deterministic delayed growth can be previewed sufficiently to feel clever rather than arbitrary. Need exact growth primitives, at least three causal families beyond "give plant more light," and a readable 15-second growth/payoff clip.

### G8C01 Maintenance Window
Prove the player is doing an interesting physical service operation rather than pausing machines on a Gantt chart. Need meaningful local repair actions whose timing changes global state. Kill if scheduling alone solves it.

### G8C07 Firebreak Foreman
Prove fire is discrete/predictable enough for reasoning yet visually alive. Need sacrificial-burn and access tradeoffs that cannot be reduced to "remove the most flammable tile first." Kill if continuous-looking simulation causes unexplained outcomes.

### G8C10 Greenhouse Night Shift
Prove ecological scheduling stays distinct from path/topology puzzles and generic farming. Need autonomous agents/plants whose ordering creates meaningful irreversible state.

### G8C03 In Case of Rain
Prove it is not generic demand allocation. Need weather to alter the physical street/customer system in ways the player can exploit, not merely forecast sales.

### G8C08 Pawnshop Stories
Prove evidence and valuation can be generated/recombined without hundreds of hand-written items. Need tests with tradeoffs and delayed feedback, not dialogue guessing.

### G8C09 Night Museum Conservator
Prove conservation state is communicated physically instead of through hidden meters. Need multiple exposure types whose interactions are causal and visually distinct without becoming a conserved-budget network.

### G8C05 Last Train Dispatcher
Prove a compact junction has enough second-order depth and a fresh surface against existing dispatch games. Need passenger/crew/platform state to matter without turning the game into timetable accounting.
